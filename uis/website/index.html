Our story

Brasaland started in Medellín in 2008 as one family-run restaurant. Over fifteen years it grew into a chain of 14 company-owned restaurants in two countries: Colombia and the United States (Florida).

The company is run from its headquarters in Medellín. Mariana Restrepo, daughter of the founder, has been CEO since 2019 and brought Brasaland to the US market. A commercial and operations office in Miami coordinates the Florida locations.
